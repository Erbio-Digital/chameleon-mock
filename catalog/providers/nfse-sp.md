# NFS-e Sao Paulo

Simulates the Sao Paulo city service invoice (NFS-e) web service surface listed below for local tests. Not affiliated with the Sao Paulo city government.

## Covered

- Invoice (NFS-e) issuance from an RPS.
- Authorization or rejection of an issued invoice.
- A callback to the application with the result.
- User and password credentials.
