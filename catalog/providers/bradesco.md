# Bradesco

Simulates the Bradesco Pix and account API surface listed below for local tests. Not affiliated with Bradesco.

## Covered

- Pix charges: immediate (cob) and due-date (cobv), create, read, pay and remove.
- Pix refunds (devolucao) with their lifecycle.
- Outgoing Pix over SPI and TED transfers.
- Balance and statement.
- Pix webhooks, delivered over mTLS.
- OAuth client credentials and client certificate (mTLS).
